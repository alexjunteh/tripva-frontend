Europe by Train 2-Week Itinerary

A rail-first route for travellers who want multiple countries without airport days. Use this as a starting point, then let Tripva build the exact day-by-day itinerary around your dates, pace, hotel area, and budget.

14 daysParis, Amsterdam, Berlin, Prague, ViennaTypical budget: $3,500-6,000

Day by day

Day 1: Paris: arrive, Le Marais and an evening Seine cruise.
Day 2: Paris: Louvre, Eiffel Tower and Montmartre.
Day 3: Paris: Musée d'Orsay, then the Eurostar/Thalys afternoon train to Amsterdam (3h20).
Day 4: Amsterdam: canal ring walk, Rijksmuseum, Jordaan cafés.
Day 5: Amsterdam: Anne Frank House (booked), bike to Vondelpark, Zaanse Schans windmills.
Day 6: Train to Berlin (6h): evening at the Brandenburg Gate and the Reichstag dome.
Day 7: Berlin: Museum Island, East Side Gallery, Kreuzberg food.
Day 8: Berlin: Topography of Terror, Checkpoint Charlie, Tiergarten.
Day 9: Train to Prague (4h20) along the Elbe valley: Old Town Square and the Astronomical Clock.
Day 10: Prague: Prague Castle and St. Vitus Cathedral, Charles Bridge at sunset.
Day 11: Prague: Vyšehrad, beer hall lunch, Petřín Hill.
Day 12: Train to Vienna (4h): Ringstrasse, St. Stephen's Cathedral, a Heuriger dinner.
Day 13: Vienna: Schönbrunn Palace, the Belvedere's Klimt rooms, café culture.
Day 14: Vienna: Naschmarkt brunch, then depart from Vienna airport.

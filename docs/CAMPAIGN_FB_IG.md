## POSTING RULES

1. **Facebook:** always include the direct link in the post body (FB supports clickable links in post copy).
2. **Instagram:** all links go to "link in bio" — update the Linktree/bio link to `tripva.app` or the relevant demo.
3. **Hashtags (IG only):** 8-15 per post. Mix broad (#travelplanning 5M+) with specific (#tripva, #aitravelplanner). No hashtags on Facebook.
4. **No emoji in body copy** — per brand guidelines. Hashtags are the visual break.
5. **One CTA per post.** Don't split between "try it" and "share it" and "comment."
6. **Respond to every comment within 4 hours during launch week.**
7. **Facebook Groups:** Post 17 is for organic group posting. Join 5-10 active travel planning groups first. One value post per group per week max — don't spam.
